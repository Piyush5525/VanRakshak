# Factory check

**Reports → Check a factory.** Place a factory on the map, describe it (or upload its PDF), and see what it does to the forest, air, water and people around it, today and 7 / 30 / 90 days after work starts.

1. **Where:** click the map (drag the pin to adjust) or use the map centre. A PDF that states coordinates can place the pin for you.
2. **Tell us about it:** type, site area, chimney height, water use, and optional emission figures (each needs its source). Or **Upload a PDF** (project report, EIA summary, permit, brochure): the text is read, and each detail found is shown with its page and sentence and filled into the form. Check every value. A scanned PDF with no text layer is reported as unreadable (no OCR). The file itself is not stored, only its fingerprint.
3. **Check the effect:** reads land cover (ESA WorldCover), rivers, villages and protected areas (OpenStreetMap), and a year of wind (Open-Meteo), then draws the site, the 300 m edge zone, smoke zones downwind, forest nearby, rivers, villages and reserves.

The result gives a High / Medium / Low rating for forest, habitat, air, water, people and carbon, each with its numbers; a **Today / 7 / 30 / 90 days** view with Play (cleared area, edge-affected forest, CO₂, forest in the smoke zone, water pathway); a wind rose; a PDF report; and **Watch real change nearby**, which runs the 90-day satellite change scan around the site.

## What is measured and what is assumed
* Measured from open data: forest inside the site and around it, distance to forest, rivers, villages (downwind or not), reserves, prevailing wind.
* Assumed and shown with the result: the site is a square of the stated area; edge effects reach 300 m; carbon density 70–150 t C/ha; smoke rings are illustrative, not a dispersion model; air and water concern by factory type is a general profile.
* The 7/30/90-day figures are a scenario: clearing takes 45 days, operation starts on day 60, edge effects reach full width by day 90, smoke reaches the downwind zone 30 days after start. Change the first two under *More details*.
* No pollutant concentration is predicted, and nothing says a project is or is not lawful. It is a screening aid, not an environmental impact assessment.
